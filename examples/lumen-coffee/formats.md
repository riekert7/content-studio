# Face or voice

Hands and voice. No faces: the roasters prefer to stay behind the counter.

# Length

20 to 35 seconds, 45 at the very most.

# Energy

Calm and deadpan, with one beat of surprise.

# The first 1.5 seconds

A hand holds a bag with a roast date from three months ago, and a voice says what is wrong with it. No greeting, no logo.
