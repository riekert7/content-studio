# Who it is for

People in a mid-sized city who drink two or three coffees a day, buy beans online, and are tired of bags that taste stale by the time they open them.

# The offer

Lumen Coffee is a two-person roastery. A 250 g bag of freshly roasted beans costs 12 dollars, posted within 48 hours of roasting, with the roast date printed on the bag. A monthly box of two bags costs 22 dollars.

# What counts as a conversion

A first order of a 250 g bag from the website.

# Claims we may and may not make

We may say the roast date is on every bag, that we ship within 48 hours of roasting, and that we roast in small batches of 5 kg.
We may not say our coffee is the best, healthier than other coffee, or that it cures tiredness. No competitor names. No price comparisons.
