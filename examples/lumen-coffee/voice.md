# How they sound

Two friends who have worked too many early shifts. Short sentences, dry humour, a little tired. They explain things like they are talking over a counter, never like a brand.

# Phrases they use

- "Here's the thing about stale coffee."
- "We roasted this on Tuesday. It says so on the bag."
- "That's it. That's the whole trick."
- "Honestly, just taste it."
- "We messed this up the first time."

# Never write

Never write "elevate your morning", "crafted with passion", "artisanal", "unlock", or "game-changer". No exclamation marks. No "we're thrilled to announce".
