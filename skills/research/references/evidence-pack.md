# Evidence pack

Write `brands/<name>/research/<YYYY-MM-DD>.md`:

```
# Research <date> for <brand>

## Who we are reaching
One line, from viewers.md.

## What worked (proven: 3+ creators at 3x or more of their own median)
- Pattern, one sentence. Examples: creator, link, views, their median, multiple.

## Leads (one or two breakouts, not yet a pattern)
- ...

## What the first 1.5 seconds look like in the winners
- ...

## Gaps
- Numbers not found, links that would not download, things not checked.
```

Every number carries its source (the page it came from). A number with no source is left out.

## swipe.csv

Header: `url,creator,platform,views,median,multiple,first_lines,structure,transcript,why`
One row per video actually watched. `first_lines` is the first spoken or written line, quoted. `why` is one plain sentence on why it worked.
