# The brand interview

Ask one question at a time. Wait for the answer. Use their own words in the file. If they do not know, write the gap down as "Not decided yet" and move on; never fill a gap for them.

## brand.md
1. Who is this for? Describe one real customer: where they are, what is going wrong for them today.
2. What do you sell, for how much, and what does the buyer get?
3. What is the one action that makes a video worth it: a sale, a booking, a DM, a follow? Pick one.
4. What can you prove about your product, and what must you never claim? (Prices, results, health, comparisons with others.)

## voice.md
5. Paste three real things you wrote or said: posts, messages or voice-note text. Real ones, not tidied up.
6. From those, list the phrases that sound like them (five to ten). Show the list and ask what to cut.
7. What would you never say? Words that feel fake, hype openers, anything you would cringe at.
8. Write "How they sound" as three or four lines from the samples. Show it and ask if it sounds like them.

## style.md
9. Brand colours (hex codes if they have them, otherwise ask for three colour words and pick hex codes together).
10. A caption font (if unsure: system bold), where captions sit, how many words at a time.
11. Does a logo ever appear on screen? "Never" is fine.

## formats.md
12. Face, hands only or voice only?
13. How long should a video be, and the longest you would ever go?
14. Energy: calm, fast, deadpan, loud?
15. What should the viewer see and hear in the first 1.5 seconds?

## viewers.md
16. Name three real or realistic viewers: name, age, what they do, what they scroll past, what makes them stop. Make the third one a sceptic.

## Closing
- Offer the permission rules: run `cs doctor --allow`, show the JSON, and say they go into `.claude/settings.json` in this project folder so Claude stops asking before each `cs` command. Write the file only if they say yes, and merge with what is already there.
- Say where the files are (`brands/<name>/`) and that they can edit them any time.
