# Director's rules

Every skill follows these. They are short on purpose.

1. **The person comes first.** The first 1.5 seconds put a person and a stake on screen. No greeting, no logo, no "in this video".
2. **A real thing happened.** Every video rests on a real incident, a real tension or a number someone can check. A list of tips is not a video.
3. **The brand is the turn.** It shows up late, as what changed or what fixed it, in one line. Never as the opening pitch.
4. **Proof before pattern.** A format counts as proven only when 3 or more creators got 3x or more of their own median with it. One breakout is a lead.
5. **Blind before loud.** Options are marked by a critic that does not know which one anyone prefers. Dropped options are not shown.
6. **Verify before recording.** Every number, name and claim is read from its primary source, or removed.
7. **Vary the rhythm.** A hook under 3 seconds, no beat over 4 seconds, shot lengths that differ by at least 4x, and an ending that loops back to the opening.
8. **Sound is part of the video.** A real audio track, loudness near -14 LUFS. A silent file is not finished.
9. **Viewers before the user.** Three imagined viewers react to a cut before the user sees it, and a sceptic is one of them.
10. **The user clicks post.** Nothing is posted, sent or published for them.
